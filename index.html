Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form above — pick your services, date, and time. You'll get an instant confirmation and we'll text you to lock it in. Prefer to talk? Just call or text (956) 202-2106.

How much will my job cost?

Pricing is listed in our Services section. Driveways and houses can vary with size and condition, so the price shown is a starting estimate — we always confirm the final quote with you before any work begins. No surprises.

Do you need access to water or electricity?

We bring our own equipment. In most cases we just need access to an outdoor water spigot. If that's an issue, let us know in the notes and we'll work it out.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. As long as we can access the area being cleaned, you don't have to be there. Add gate codes, pet info, or any special instructions in the notes when you book.

How long does it take?

Most jobs are completed the same day. Trash cans take minutes, driveways around an hour, and houses typically one to three hours depending on size.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Prestige Power Washing is licensed and insured, and every job is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee — if you're not happy, we'll make it right.
