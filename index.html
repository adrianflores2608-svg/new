About Us

A Sanctuary of Style & Self-Care

At RGV Luxury Nails, we believe every visit should feel like a retreat. Our talented team of licensed nail technicians and beauty specialists bring artistry and precision to every service — from classic manicures to stunning acrylic sets and relaxing pedicures.

Located in the heart of Harlingen, TX, we're committed to using premium products in a clean, welcoming environment. Your comfort and satisfaction are our top priority.

What We Offer

Our Services

Indulge in our full menu of nail and beauty services, crafted to make you look and feel your best.

💅

Acrylic Nails

Full sets and fills with a flawless, long-lasting finish. Customized shapes and lengths to suit your style.

✨

Basic & Deluxe Manicures

Classic nail care with shaping, cuticle work, and your choice of polish — or upgrade to a deluxe treatment.

🌟

Shellac

Chip-resistant gel-polish hybrid that lasts up to three weeks with a brilliant, high-gloss shine.

💎

Dip Nails

Durable, lightweight dip powder nails for a natural look with lasting color and added strength.

🌿

Keratin Treatment Nails

Nourishing keratin-infused nail treatments that strengthen and condition for healthier-looking nails.

🛁

Pedicures

Relaxing foot soaks, exfoliation, and nail care that leave your feet soft, smooth, and beautifully polished.

🌸

Waxing

Gentle and effective waxing services for smooth, hair-free skin — brows, lip, chin, and more.

Eyelash Extensions

Eyelash Perm

Brow Laminations
