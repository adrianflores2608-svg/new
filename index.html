What We Offer

Our Services

From classic manicures to stunning lash extensions — we do it all with precision and care.

💅

Acrylic Nails

Full sets and fills with flawless, long-lasting results. Custom shapes, lengths, and designs.

✨

Basic & Deluxe Manicures

Shaping, cuticle care, and polish in a classic or upgraded deluxe treatment.

💎

Shellac

Chip-resistant gel-polish that lasts up to three weeks with a brilliant high-gloss shine.

🌟

Dip Nails

Lightweight dip powder for a natural look with lasting color and added nail strength.

🌿

Keratin Treatment Nails

Nourishing keratin-infused treatments that strengthen and condition for healthier nails.

Pedicures

Relaxing foot soaks, exfoliation, and nail care — leaving your feet smooth and polished.

Waxing

👁️

Eyelash Extensions

Semi-permanent lashes applied individually for a full, gorgeous look lasting weeks.

🔆

Eyelash Perm

A lift and curl treatment giving your natural lashes a beautifully wide-eyed effect.

💫

Brow Laminations

Define and restructure your brows for a full, feathered, perfectly groomed look.
