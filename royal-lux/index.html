Heritage

The Atelier

Hand-Crafted, Never Hurried.

A single Royal Lux timepiece passes through the hands of eleven artisans across four months. Our atelier is not a factory — it is a conservatory of techniques refined over generations.

Bespoke commissions are welcomed. Each client is paired with a dedicated Directeur de Création to guide the process from first sketch to final polish.
